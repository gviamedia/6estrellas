GTA 6 safe codes: why online lists don't work

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamSingle source

Short answer

No list will work: according to GameSpot, GTA 6 safe codes are randomized, changing every playthrough and even if you rob the same store twice. The code is on a guard or somewhere nearby, and smaller safes can be picked.

How to find the code

  • Some safes need a code. According to GameSpot, a guard usually carries it or it's written down nearby; Focus mode (click both sticks) helps you spot it.
  • If you can't find it, smaller safes can be picked and lockboxes snapped open.
  • Most safes can be forced open, but that usually trips the alarm.

What about Brooks Boatworks or Kingpin Bowling?

They're among the most searched safe codes right now. GameSpot doesn't mention any exceptions to codes being randomized, so a code you find online probably won't match your game: search the location itself.

Before you open the safe

Handle the crowd, disable the alarm and destroy the camera footage first. Every step is in how to rob a store without tripping the alarm.

Safe codes aren't cheats: GTA 6 cheats are entered on the phone and only work after the story.

How do you keep a car in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Register it at a Pay 'n' Spray. You start with room for only three personal vehicles; buy garages for more.

Registering stolen cars

According to German site PC Games, stolen cars can be repaired, resprayed and re-registered at a Pay 'n' Spray so you can keep them. Unregistered cars show up gray on the radar and disappear after a restart.

Space

You start with space for three vehicles and can buy garages to expand, per PC Games in a pre-launch article (September 8, 2026) citing a Famitsu interview with Rockstar.

Getting your car delivered

A quick request to Scooter Bros gets your car delivered, according to Push Square.

Sell instead of keep

Don't want it? Pay 'n' Spray will buy it, per GamesRadar. It's one of the ways to make money early.

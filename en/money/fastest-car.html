What's the fastest car in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamSingle source

Short answer

It can't be said for sure yet. GTABase, which tests cars in-game, hasn't finished its measurements; its provisional November 19 ranking puts the Pegassi Infernus '96 first, followed by the Grotti Cheetah '95 and the Pfister Comet '88.

Provisional ranking by top speed

  1. Pegassi Infernus '96: 7.1 out of 10
  2. Grotti Cheetah '95: 6.6 out of 10
  3. Pfister Comet '88: 6.1 out of 10

These are scores out of 10, not mph. GTABase lists 30 cars and warns the order isn't final: it will add positions and figures as it finishes testing each car.

Another ranking from the same site

In its supercar list, sorted by overall stats, GTABase puts the Pfister Comet '88, the Obey 8F Drafter and the Annis Elegy '90 on top, and says it's testing every vehicle in-game. That list shows no date.

How to get them

GTABase still marks how to get almost every car as “coming soon,” and we haven't found a second reliable source. To hold on to a car you already have, see garages and Pay 'n' Spray. The Grotti Cheetah '95 is also part of the Ultimate Edition, per Rockstar (see editions).

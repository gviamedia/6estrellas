Six Stars Editorial Team

Six Stars is an independent, English-language companion to 6 Estrellas, a Spanish site that answers the specific questions players run into in GTA 6, in a few lines. Six Stars Editorial Team is the byline we publish everything under: we don't sign articles with personal names.

Who writes this

Every article is published under the “Six Stars Editorial Team” byline. We have no connection to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

We don't run our own tests in the game. Everything we publish is based on cited sources: Rockstar's announcements and support pages, the PlayStation and Xbox stores, and established outlets. We never present anything as checked by us in-game.

How we verify information

  • Answer first. Every page opens with the short answer, with the details below.
  • Sources in plain sight. Every page links its sources and shows when it was last reviewed. We prefer official sources; when only the press has reported something, we say so.
  • In our own words. We don't copy other sites: we summarize and link the source.
  • Doubts flagged. We separate what's confirmed from what isn't, and when two sources disagree, we say so.

What the labels mean

Each article carries a label next to its date:

  • Per cited sources: the linked sources agree with what we say.
  • Partly confirmed: part of the answer isn't confirmed yet; the page says which.
  • Single source: the claim rests on one source and we haven't found a second.
  • Figure may change: the number changes over time (review scores, download sizes). Check the date.
  • Rough estimate: an approximate figure from the sources, not official data.

Corrections

If something is wrong or a question is missing, email contacto@6estrellas.org. When we fix something, we update the page's review date.

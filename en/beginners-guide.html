GTA 6 beginner's guide: 12 things to know before you start

Version of November 20, 2026Free

In short

Save to separate slots often, keep your money in the bank, register the cars you want to keep and don't count on cheats until you've beaten the story.

  1. You can't replay missions

    Save to a new slot before every mission with a choice. GamesRadar reports 15 manual slots, with autosave kept separate.

    More details

  2. Switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks around Chapters 2 and 3

    Until then, the game decides who you play. After that, you can switch freely unless the other character is busy.

    More details

  3. Cheats unlock after the story

    Most also need a challenge, and using them turns off trophies and saving.

    More details

  4. You lose your cash when you die

    Move money into your BuckMe account. Don't put anything you'll need into Family Savings: it can't come back out.

    More details

  5. Register the cars you want to keep

    Stolen cars are registered at Pay 'n' Spray. You start with room for three.

    More details

  6. First money: other criminals, fences, Bret and the derby

    Robbing crooks mid-crime, selling stolen cars, hunting and the Ambrosia demolition derby pay best early on.

    More details

  7. The cops remember your clothes

    Changing your outfit and getaway car helps you shake them. Non-lethal shots keep your criminal profile cleaner.

    More details

  8. There's more than one ending

    Your choices matter, including Jason and Lucia's relationship.

    More details

  9. English voices, subtitles in other languages

    Voice acting is English only. Turn up subtitle size if you drive a lot.

    More details

  10. 30fps on every console

    No 40 or 60fps mode. PS5 Pro wins on sharpness and stability, not frame rate.

    More details

  11. No online or PC version yet

    Neither has a date. Any “PC download” is a scam.

    More details

  12. Won't start? Check the network first

    Check PSN or Xbox status, update your console and make sure you have up to 212 GB free.

    More details

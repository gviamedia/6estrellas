How many endings does GTA 6 have?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

More than one, and your choices matter. Kotaku saw two endings; the total hasn't been confirmed.

What's confirmed

There is more than one ending: Kotaku saw two in its review. Jason and Lucia's relationship can be platonic or romantic depending on your choices, also per Kotaku. Push Square cautions that some decisions feel more like an illusion than something truly meaningful.

An early example

Early on you can decorate the beach house. It's optional, and according to Push Square, finishing it in time brings the couple closer, while skipping it means Lucia notices you didn't go the extra mile. It isn't clear whether it can be missed for good.

How to protect your run

Because missions can't be replayed, save to a new slot before any mission with a choice. It's the only safe way to try the other option.

Still unconfirmed: the exact number of endings and whether you can redo an ending from the same save.

What weapons are in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamSingle source

Short answer

GTABase lists 205 confirmed weapons plus 24 equipment items and 5 ammo types, and says more are still to be found. Guns you've bought can be retrieved from Ammu-Nation lockers or your personal vehicle.

Categories

GTABase's database groups weapons into assault rifles, pistols, shotguns, SMGs, melee, sniper rifles, tactical rifles, light machine guns, launchers and throwables.

Ammo types listed: heavy, shotgun, pistol, spear and rockets.

Where your guns are stored

  • According to GTABase, weapons you've bought or own aren't lost for good and can be picked up from any Ammu-Nation locker or your personal vehicle.
  • Some Ammu-Nation locations carry unique regional weapons.

What's the best gun?

We haven't found a reliable ranking made with the released game; the ones circulating predate launch. This is a single source, and GTABase itself says its list covers weapons known “so far.”

Does GTA 6 autosave? How to save your game

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

From the pause menu, almost any time. GamesRadar and Vandal report 15 manual save slots, and GamesRadar adds a separate autosave slot. Rockstar hasn't detailed this itself in what we've checked.

How to save

  1. Press Start to open the game menu.
  2. Select Game.
  3. Pick a slot.
  4. Choose Save Game.

That's the process as described by GamesRadar. You can overwrite or load any of the 15 manual slots.

How autosave works

GamesRadar describes a separate, 16th slot reserved for autosave. It's overwritten after missions, when you're Wasted or Busted, and after major open-world encounters.

When you can't save

  • During a mission: you rely on autosave, though you can restart from the beginning or a checkpoint.
  • Right after a call or objective: if free roam won't let you save, leave the building or vehicle and try again.
  • With cheats on: GamesRadar, PowerPyx and Vandal all say any cheat disables saving until you quit and reload.

There's also no in-game quit option, per GamesRadar: if you close the app from the console home screen without saving, you're back to your last autosave.

Tip

Since you can't replay missions, keep one slot for your main run and save to a new one before any mission with a choice.

What is GTA 6's review score?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamFigure may change

Short answer

A 99 on Metacritic, based on launch-day data. The average shifts as new reviews come in.

The average

TheWrap reported a 99 on Metacritic based on 147 reviews on November 18, 2026. Tom's Guide's live roundup also cites 99 after about 150 reviews, and Spanish outlet Vandal counted 153 reviews on PS5 the next day, noting that the score ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

We haven't found a reliable source for an OpenCritic score, so we don't list one.

Some scores

  • 10/10: IGN (per TheWrap), Push Square and Vandal.
  • No score: Kotaku, which doesn't score games.

Averages move as more reviews are published. These figures are from launch week.

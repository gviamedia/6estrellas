How many trophies and achievements does GTA 6 have?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

According to GTABase, 55 trophies on PS5 and 55 achievements on Xbox, with a Platinum called “Vice Got a Price!” Several outlets trace the count to a data leak, and we haven't found a list published by Rockstar.

What the sources say

  • PS5: 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze.
  • Xbox Series X|S: 55 achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore. The PlayStation Platinum maps to a “Mythic” achievement that awards no points.
  • Platinum: “Vice Got a Price!”, unlocked by earning everything else.

These details come from GTABase's guide, last updated November 18, 2026. According to that guide, 28 of them are hidden.

What isn't confirmed

Notebookcheck and Insider Gaming reported the 55 figure as a data leak, and GTABase itself marks the exact steps for several achievements as unconfirmed. We haven't found an official list from Rockstar, so the total and names could change.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

No. Unlike GTA V, GTA 6 has no option to replay a mission once you've finished it. The only way back is to load an older save.

This is one of the biggest surprises for GTA V players, who could replay any mission from the pause menu to chase better scores. GamesRadar, Vandal (citing Eurogamer) and GTABase all report that GTA 6 has no mission replay, and GamesRadar thinks it is unlikely to be added later.

What happened to gold medals?

GTA 6 drops per-mission Gold Medal objectives, according to GTABase. GamesRadar says the old skill challenges now live in their own section of the Progress menu, are much broader, and are mainly used to unlock cheats after you finish the story.

The workaround: multiple save slots

GTA 6 gives you 15 manual save slots. If you want to revisit a specific moment, save to a fresh slot before the mission starts and don't overwrite it. Always do this before missions with choices.

See how saving and autosave work. If Rockstar ever adds mission replay in an update, we'll update this page.

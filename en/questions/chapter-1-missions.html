What are the missions in GTA 6? Full list by chapter and missables

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

The story has a Prologue and five chapters. GTABase numbers 122 missions including optional ones, 27 of them in Chapter 1, from “Gone Fishing” to “Discharge Time.” Four optional missions can be missed: two in Chapter 1, one in Chapter 3 and one in Chapter 4.

Prologue

A single mission, also called “Prologue.”

Optional activities

GTABase also lists optional tasks in Chapter 1, not marked as missable: Pick Up Groceries, Buy Flowers, Buy Lucia a Necklace, Get The Chop, Get Lucia's Clothes, Find the Lost Drugs, Clock Out and Donnie's Lost Drugs.

Which ones can you miss?

GTABase flags “Get Shirley's Rum” and “Steal the Scooter” as optional and missable. Spanish outlet Vandal's guide, which also lists them, orders the chapter slightly differently, so treat the exact order as provisional. Since missions can't be replayed, save first: see how to save.

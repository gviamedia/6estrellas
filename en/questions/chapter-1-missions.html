What are the Chapter 1 missions in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

GTA 6's story has a Prologue and five chapters. GTABase's launch-week list puts 27 missions in Chapter 1, from “Gone Fishing” to “Discharge Time.” Two optional missions can be missed for good.

Prologue

A single mission, also called “Prologue.”

Chapter 1 story missions

Titles only, in GTABase's order (it skips number 24):

  1. Gone Fishing
  2. Gear Up
  3. Unwanted Attention
  4. Pipework
  5. Lab Fees
  6. Just a Scratch
  7. Get Shirley's Rum (optional, missable)
  8. Steal the Scooter (optional, missable)
  9. Hey Arnold!
  10. Island Dreams
  11. Feng Shui
  12. Coming Home
  13. Clean Up After Donnie
  14. Buck Me
  15. Down to Earth
  16. Deposit Cash Into BuckMe
  17. Save for the Future
  18. Day Release
  19. Clear Skies
  20. Building a Life
  21. Swamped
  22. Don Gone
  1. Recalibration
  2. Tourist Trap
  3. Home Sweet Home
  4. Discharge Time

Optional activities

GTABase also lists optional tasks in Chapter 1, not marked as missable: Pick Up Groceries, Buy Flowers, Buy Lucia a Necklace, Get The Chop, Get Lucia's Clothes, Find the Lost Drugs, Clock Out and Donnie's Lost Drugs.

Which ones can you miss?

GTABase flags “Get Shirley's Rum” and “Steal the Scooter” as optional and missable. Spanish outlet Vandal's guide, which also lists them, orders the chapter slightly differently, so treat the exact order as provisional. Since missions can't be replayed, save first: see how to save.

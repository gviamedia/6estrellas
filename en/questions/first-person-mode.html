Does GTA 6 have a first-person mode?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Not a full one at launch. You only switch to first person while aiming with certain weapons.

GTA V lets you play the whole game in first person on its newer versions. GTA 6 doesn't at launch. Rob Nelson of Rockstar North said so in an interview with YouTuber Davy Jones, as reported by VICE before release.

What you do get is a first-person perspective while aiming with certain weapons.

Will it come later?

Nothing has been announced. In GTA V, first-person mode arrived with the PS4 and Xbox One release in November 2014, more than a year after the original 2013 launch, but that guarantees nothing for GTA 6.

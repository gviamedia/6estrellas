Can you buy houses or businesses in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

There's no reliable confirmation yet that you can buy houses or businesses. Homes come through the story. What you can buy is garages.

Homes

The homes we know about come with the story. Jason, for example, lives rent-free in one of Brian Heder's properties in the Leonida Keys in exchange for helping with local shakedowns, according to VICE, which also says Rockstar hasn't confirmed whether houses can be bought. Push Square mentions a beach house and the pair's Vice City penthouse.

At a safehouse you can grab something from the fridge, trim or shave your hair (a new style needs a barber or salon) and switch characters when the story allows it, per VICE.

Garages

You start with room for just three personal vehicles and can buy garages for more space, according to German site PC Games in a pre-launch article citing a Famitsu interview with Rockstar. More in how to keep a car.

What about businesses?

We haven't found a reliable post-launch source confirming businesses you can buy. GTA+ members get a Crosstown garage with an armory and a place to sell stolen goods, according to Vandal's launch coverage (see what GTA+ includes).

Still unconfirmed by reliable sources: whether houses or businesses can be bought. We'll update this page as soon as that changes.

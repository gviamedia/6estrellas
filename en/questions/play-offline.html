Can you play GTA 6 offline?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

Yes. It's a single-player game and the PlayStation Store lists it as supporting offline play. But you need internet to download it, even with the physical edition.

Playing offline

The PlayStation Store listing marks GTA 6 as compatible with offline play. The Xbox Store notes that some features may require an internet connection and signing up for a Rockstar Games account.

Downloading requires internet

The physical edition has no disc: the box contains a download code, according to Rockstar. You always have to download the full game, and Rockstar says it needs up to 212 GB of free space depending on your console. See GTA 6 download size.

If the game won't start because of a network problem, check what to do when GTA 6 won't load. Looking for multiplayer? Here's the status of GTA 6 Online.

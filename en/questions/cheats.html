Does GTA 6 have cheats? All codes and how to unlock them

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

Yes, 61 of them, with the same codes on PS5 and Xbox. They only work after you beat the story and meet each cheat's own requirement. As of November 20, 19 codes are known; all 61 cheats and their requirements are below.

This is the big change from earlier GTA games: cheats aren't available from the start. PowerPyx, Red Bull and Vandal agree that they unlock after you complete the main story and that each one also has its own requirement, listed under Progress > Cheats. Enter a code before that and nothing happens.

According to Red Bull, the game counts what you've already done during the story, so some cheats may be ready the moment the credits roll. It pays to work on the requirements as you play.

How to enter a cheat

  1. Press up on the d-pad to pull out your phone.
  2. Hold L1 and press R1 (on Xbox, hold LB and press RB).
  3. Enter the cheat's d-pad sequence.

Codes only use the d-pad, so they're identical on PS5 and Xbox. Several look alike (the ammo cheats share an opening, and vehicle spawns all start with ← ↑ → ↓): one wrong press gets you a different cheat or none.

What you give up

  • Trophies and achievements are disabled.
  • Saving is disabled until you quit and reload (see how to save). Save before you use them.

All 61 cheats and how to unlock each one

There are 10 player cheats, 11 combat cheats, 21 vehicle spawns, 3 environment cheats and 16 screen filters. All three guides agree on the requirements; as of November 20, 19 codes are known. Unless noted, a code appears in all three. “Not found yet” means the requirement is official but nobody has published the sequence.

Watch out for fake lists

  • There are no phone-number cheats. Red Bull says “GTA 6 cheat phone numbers” lists are GTA V codes with a new label; they don't work in GTA 6.
  • Safe combinations aren't cheats and aren't fixed. GameSpot says they're randomized per playthrough, even if you rob the same store twice, so online “safe code” lists won't help. See how to find them in how to make money.

We'll add the missing codes as reliable sources publish them.

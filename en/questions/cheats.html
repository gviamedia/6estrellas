Does GTA 6 have cheats? How to unlock them

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Yes, 61 of them, with the same inputs on PS5 and Xbox. But they only work after you beat the main story, and most also require completing a challenge.

This is the big change from earlier GTA games: cheats aren't available from the start. PowerPyx, Red Bull and Vandal all say they unlock after you complete the main story, and most also need a specific challenge (Progress menu > Cheats). Enter a code before that and nothing happens.

How to enter a cheat

  1. Press up on the d-pad to pull out your phone.
  2. Hold L1 and press R1 (on Xbox, hold LB and press RB).
  3. Enter the cheat's d-pad sequence.

What you give up

  • Trophies and achievements are disabled.
  • Saving is disabled until you quit and reload (see how to save).

The 61 figure is the number of cheats; many of the actual codes were still undiscovered on launch day. We'll only list codes once reliable sources confirm them.

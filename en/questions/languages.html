What languages is GTA 6 in?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Voices are English only, as in previous GTA games. Menus and subtitles come in several languages, including Spanish for Spain and for Mexico.

GTA 6 sticks with series tradition: no dubbed versions. The PlayStation Store lists the voice language as English only.

Text and subtitles

  • The Xbox Store listing says 13 languages are supported, without naming them.
  • The PlayStation Store lists 12 screen languages, including Spanish and Spanish (Mexico).

Spanish outlet Vandal, reading the store listing back in June, reported the same: English audio only, with Spanish text for Spain and Mexico.

Tip

If it's hard to follow dialogue while driving, increase the subtitle size in the game's display or accessibility settings.

Sources

  • PlayStation Store listing for GTA VI (Spain)
  • Xbox Store listing for GTA VI (Spain)
  • Vandal (Spanish): GTA 6 keeps English-only voices (June 29, 2026)

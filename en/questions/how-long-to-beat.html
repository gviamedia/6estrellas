How long is GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamRough estimate

Short answer

The main story takes roughly 50 to 80 hours depending on who's counting and how much you wander. Estimates for 100% completion run from about 200 to 300+ hours.

What Rockstar said

Rob Nelson of Rockstar North told The New York Times that a full run of the main story took him about 80 hours, roughly double GTA V. He was describing a playthrough during development in February, as reported by Spanish outlet Vandal in August 2026, so the final game may differ.

What reviewers measured

  • Push Square: a roughly 60-hour storyline that will likely take the average player around 80 hours. The reviewer played over 150 hours.
  • Kotaku: 152 hours played, main campaign finished and two endings seen.
  • 3DJuegos (Spanish): about 55 hours if you stick to the story.
  • MeriStation (Spanish), via Demócrata: 70 hours for the five chapters and 95 story missions, including some exploration; about 50 hours for the campaign alone.

What about 100%?

MeriStation's figure is about 200 hours. Push Square says a 100% save could take up to 300 hours or more; that's a single reviewer's estimate and depends on whether you use guides. Treat both as rough.

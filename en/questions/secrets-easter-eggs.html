What secrets and Easter eggs are in GTA 6? Everything found so far

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

In the first two days, players found an alien abduction with its own trophy, a literal Easter egg atop Vice City's tallest tower, Bigfoot, Dexter Morgan's apartment and nods to Red Dead Redemption, among others. Rockstar doesn't confirm secrets: all of this comes from press coverage and players.

Rockstar doesn't confirm secrets, so everything below comes from outlets that checked it or from player videos they reported on. When only one source has it, we say so.

Alien abduction (with a trophy)

According to GameSpot, which lays out the steps, and The Tab:

  1. Finish Chapter 1.
  2. Alone and after 2 a.m. in-game, head northwest of the Starlet Motel in Kelly County (near Port Gellhorn).
  3. Look for a stranded car with flashing headlights: it points to a trail into the woods, where you'll see a blue light.
  4. Offer to help the woman being abducted and fetch her car. Then you get abducted.

It unlocks the “The truth is out there” trophy or achievement.

The follow-up: the secret base

According to GameSpot, some time later the abducted character has a dream with clues to a rescue mission at South Point Airfield in Hamlet: you get into the radio tower building through the roof and use a keypad. GameSpot says the code is “usually” 2134 and is shown in the dream; other sites give a different combination, so watch the dream in your own game. There's radiation and toxic gas inside, so move fast.

A literal Easter egg

There's a nest at the top of the tallest tower in Vice City. GameSpot and GamingBible say the player who found it took about seven minutes to climb; up top you can grab an egg and throw it, and we won't spoil what happens next. GamingBible describes it as a communications tower, so the exact spot depends on who's telling it.

Bigfoot

GameSpot places him in Gullet Marsh and says he's tied to a mission, so he's hard to miss. The Tab points to the wetlands south of Hamlet.

Dexter Morgan's apartment

It's on Washington Island in Vice City, near the Top Fun Drones shop in Vice Beach, per GameSpot. You can't go inside. Some players think the boat docked nearby looks like the show's Slice of Life.

Nods to Red Dead and Liberty City

  • A Western novel about a cowboy from New Austin, a Red Dead Redemption region; GameSpot spotted it in Jacob's Tavern in Millport.
  • On the table in Jason's house early in Chapter 1, a former Liberty City mobster's memoir references 2008, the year GTA IV is set.

You can read excerpts from books and magazines in the game.

The secret radio station

The Vault Radio unlocks by scanning a poster inside The Vault nightclub in Vice City. Details in GTA 6 radio stations.

Unconfirmed

  • A lifeguard dog: a dog in sunglasses on a jet ski that rescues stranded swimmers, around Partridge Bay and Gambit Bay according to Playday. GameSpot has also reported on the dog.
  • Michael's boat: some players think a boat parked by Jason's house is Michael's from GTA V, per Game Rant. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

This is a living page: we'll add more secrets as sources confirm them.

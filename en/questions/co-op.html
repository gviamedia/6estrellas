Does GTA 6 have co-op?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

No co-op has been confirmed. Rockstar hasn't announced a co-op mode for the story, and the game launched as a single-player experience with no online mode.

The closest thing to co-op

The closest thing shown officially is switching between the two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, as the story progresses. Details in how to switch characters.

Rumors

Some leakers have talked about a two-player campaign. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it, so we treat it as a rumor.

And online?

GTA 6 launched as a single-player experience and Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode or a date. See the current status of GTA 6 Online.

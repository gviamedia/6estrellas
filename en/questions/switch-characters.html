How do you switch between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

Not at first. Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks somewhere around Chapters 2 and 3 (guides disagree on the exact point). Until then, the story decides who you play.

You can't swap at the start. VGC and GamesRadar say you first take Lucia into the open world after Chapter 1 and that free switching turns on at a set point in Chapter 2. Spanish outlet 3DJuegos instead places it in Chapter 3, after a Chapter 2 played only as Lucia. Either way, it arrives early in the story.

How to switch

It's on the d-pad, but guides describe the input differently: VGC says press right on the d-pad, while GamesRadar says hold right and press R1 (RB on Xbox). Check the controls menu in your own game.

When you can't switch

  • If the other character is busy (for example, riding in a RydeMe), their portrait is grayed out.
  • Missions marked with a J or an L on the map can only be played by that character.
  • When you're together, your partner can drive, cover you during robberies or head off on their own.

Kotaku adds that your partner's life carries on off-screen while you aren't controlling them.

How do you lose the cops in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Police remember what you look like: they share your description over the radio and the game keeps a criminal profile of how you behave. Changing your clothes and your getaway car helps you slip past them.

What's new compared with GTA V

  • Criminal profile: the game tracks how you behave, and that changes how police and the wider world treat you (Push Square, Kotaku).
  • Radio descriptions: officers share details about Jason and Lucia with each other over the radio, so your look matters (Kotaku).
  • Clothes: Kotaku says you can change clothes if you have heat, and Push Square says a change of clothes and getaway car can let you slip past the police undetected.
  • Non-lethal shots: you can target non-vital areas to incapacitate people without killing them (Push Square). Kotaku notes that a violent streak can get you labeled a psycho by the criminal profile system.

What follows from that

Getting out of sight and changing your look works better than fleeing at full speed in the same car and outfit. This page summarizes what reviews describe; we'll expand it as reliable guides cover the wanted system in more detail.

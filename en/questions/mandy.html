Who is Mandy in GTA 6, and can you romance her?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Mandy is one of Brian Heder's tenants in Key Lento: Jason meets her in “Pipework,” a Chapter 1 mission where he collects her rent. She flirts with Jason, but you can't romance her. She's played by Lexie Shoaibi.

Who she is

She's one of Brian Heder's tenants in Key Lento, in the Leonida Keys. Jason meets her in “Pipework,” a Chapter 1 mission where Brian sends him to collect overdue rent and he ends up fixing her pipes, according to GTABase and GameSpot. The official credits list Lexie Shoaibi as Mandy.

Can you romance Mandy?

No. According to GameSpot, Mandy flirts with Jason throughout the mission, but the dialogue never lets you steer things toward romance, and at the end Jason leaves without the player getting a choice. You can drop by her house later and pick things back up, but that doesn't make her a romance option.

The game's only relationship is Jason and Lucia's, and Rockstar has said it's optional: if you want it to work, you have to nurture it. More in endings and choices.

Is Mandy a serial killer?

That's a player theory based on items in her home and car. It isn't confirmed. GameSpot mentions Jason pulls a “curious item” out of her pipes, but nothing more.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamFigure may change

Short answer

Up to 212 GB of free space, depending on your console. The game unlocked at midnight local time on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox.

Space

Rockstar says GTA 6 requires up to 212 GB of available space depending on your device. Forbes also reported updates before launch:

  • November 13: about 1 GB on PS5 and 2.2 GB on Xbox.
  • November 16: about 600 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox.

Preload

Digital preload began on November 10: at midnight local time on PS5, and globally at 12 a.m. GMT on Xbox (7 p.m. ET on November 9).

Unlock time

Rockstar released the game at midnight local time on November 19 in all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. If a store showed November 18, that's a time zone conversion.

No disc in the box: see the physical edition.

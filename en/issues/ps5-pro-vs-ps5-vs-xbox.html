GTA 6 on PS5 Pro vs PS5 vs Xbox: where does it look best?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

It targets 30fps on every console, with no performance mode. Digital Foundry rates PS5 Pro as the best option and Series S as the hardest to recommend.

What's confirmed

  • Rockstar says the game targets 30 frames per second (Kotaku).
  • There's no 40fps mode on any console, PS5 Pro included (Vandal).
  • On PS5 Pro, the game uses PSSR upscaling and ray tracing (Kotaku).

What Digital Foundry measured

As summarized by AltChar and Pure Xbox:

ConsoleImageStability
PS5 ProUpscaled from 1440p with PSSR; looks like native 4KNear-perfect 30fps
PS51440p internal, upscaledDips to 23fps in explosions
Xbox Series XSimilar to PS5Dips in chases and explosions
Xbox Series S720p internal, upscaled to 1080pOften 25 to 30fps, with drops to 20

Digital Foundry calls Series S the hardest version to recommend. On Xbox One, see cloud play.

Why does GTA 6 look blurry?

Internal resolution. Per Digital Foundry's analysis, the game renders internally at 1440p on PS5 and upscales, while Series S starts from 720p to reach 1080p, so it can look softer than on PS5 Pro, which upscales with PSSR. No in-game setting changes that, since every console runs at 30fps.

Is a PS5 Pro worth it just for this?

You gain sharpness and stability, not frame rate: the target is still 30. If you already own a PS5 or Series X, the game holds up well.

Does the GTA 6 physical edition come with a disc?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

No. The physical edition is a box with a download code. You still have to download the whole game.

Rockstar's support site says the physical version contains a download code and that no disc is included. It was expected in stores for shipping and pickup from November 12, and codes could be redeemed right away to preload.

What that means

  • You need internet and up to 212 GB free.
  • Only the Standard Edition is sold physically; the Ultimate Edition is digital only, though Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade.
  • Physical pre-orders included the Vintage Vice City Pack.
  • PlayStation codes are region-locked to your account's region; Xbox codes aren't.

Buying it as a gift?

If it's for someone whose PlayStation account is registered in another country, check the code's region first. Xbox doesn't have that problem.

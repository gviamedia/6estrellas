Who voices Jason and Lucia? GTA 6 voice actors and cast

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

According to Rockstar's official credits, Jason Duval is played by Dylan Rourke and Lucia Caminos by Manni L. Perez. The cast also includes Stephen Root (Brian Heder), Bobby Moynihan (Cal) and Lexie Shoaibi (Mandy). Michael Hollick, who played Niko Bellic in GTA IV, is credited as “The Hitman.”

Rockstar published GTA 6's full credits on its official site at launch. The same performers handled voice and motion capture, according to Screen Rant.

Jason and Lucia

  • Jason Duval: Dylan Rourke. Screen Rant says it's his video game debut; he's had TV guest spots on Station 19, Modern Family and 9-1-1: Lone Star.
  • Lucia Caminos: Manni L. Perez. Not her first GTA: per Screen Rant, she appeared as a clubgoer in GTA V and GTA Online, and has guest-starred on The Blacklist, Jessica Jones and Chicago P.D.

Who they are in the story: Jason and Lucia, the protagonists.

Is Niko Bellic in GTA 6?

Not confirmed. What we do know: the official credits list Michael Hollick, the actor who played Niko Bellic in GTA IV, in the role of “The Hitman.” Rockstar doesn't say that character is Niko, so we don't assume it. GameSpot also found an in-game memoir by a former Liberty City mobster that references 2008, the year GTA IV is set.

What about Mandy?

She's played by Lexie Shoaibi. Who she is and whether you can date her: Mandy in GTA 6.

Who are Jason and Lucia, GTA 6's protagonists?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Jason Duval is a military veteran doing jobs for drug runners in the Keys; Lucia Caminos is fresh out of prison. Together they try to get ahead in Leonida.

Jason Duval

  • Background: grew up around grifters and crooks.
  • Past: served in the military.
  • Now: lives in the Leonida Keys, working for local drug runners.
  • What he wants: a quiet life, which he never gets.

Rockstar frames meeting Lucia as a turn that could be the best or worst thing to happen to him.

Lucia Caminos

  • Background: her family is from Liberty City; her father taught her to fight as a kid.
  • Past: ended up in prison in Leonida for protecting her family, and got out by sheer luck.
  • What she wants: the better life she and her mother hoped for. She sees a way out in Jason.

How they get along during the game partly depends on you: see endings and choices.

Who voices them

According to the official credits, Jason is played by Dylan Rourke and Lucia by Manni L. Perez. The full cast is in GTA 6 voice actors and cast.

Other official characters

Rockstar also introduces Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder.

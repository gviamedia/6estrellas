What does GTA+ include in GTA 6?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

Rockstar confirmed GTA+ now includes GTA 6 bonuses. According to Vandal's launch coverage, they include an exclusive Elegy, a Crosstown garage with an armory, Scooter Bros Premium, free public transit and 10% off vehicle sales.

Member benefits, per Vandal

  • An exclusive '90 Annis Elegy with custom bodywork.
  • A Crosstown garage with an armory and a place to sell stolen goods directly.
  • Scooter Bros Premium.
  • Free rides on Leonida's public transit.
  • 10% on vehicle sales, plus similar discounts at several Leonida stores (Vandal doesn't say which).

Rockstar's November 19 launch post confirms GTA+ “now contains GTAVI bonuses for Members” but doesn't list them, so the details above rely on Vandal.

The free month with purchase

According to Rockstar, digital purchases before November 20, 2026 included one month of GTA+. On Xbox, the store listing we checked gives 180 days from the pre-order to redeem it. After that it renews as a paid membership, so check auto-renewal in your account if you don't want to keep it.

Is it worth it?

You don't need it for the story. It saves time with the garage and getting around. If you're unsure, start without it and see how to make money early.

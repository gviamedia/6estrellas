How much is GTA 6? Standard vs Ultimate Edition

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

In the US, the Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99, according to Take-Two's pre-order announcement.

Editions

  • Standard ($79.99): the game. Sold digitally and as a physical box with a download code (no disc).
  • Ultimate ($99.99): digital only. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately at any time.

What the Ultimate Edition adds

Rockstar lists, among other items: the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, personalized weapon variants, Jason's safehouse vehicles, the Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's mod shops, Sara's Unisex Salon, the Stock 305 clothing store, the Electric Fang tattoo parlor, the PTT Youngin$ compound and a classic car collection. Rockstar says the bonuses are spread across the story, with new items in each chapter.

Pre-order bonuses

Digital purchases before November 20, 2026 included the Vintage Vice City Pack ('55 Vapid Stanier sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, an exclusive weapon pattern) and a month of GTA+. Physical pre-orders included the Vintage Vice City Pack but not GTA+. Rockstar's launch post called November 19 the last day.

Rating

Rockstar's posts list these content descriptors: Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, Use of Drugs and Alcohol, plus In-Game Purchases. In Europe it's rated PEGI 18.

Prices can change with sales. Always check the current price in the store.

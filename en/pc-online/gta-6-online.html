Does GTA 6 have an online mode?

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPer cited sources

Short answer

No. GTA 6 launched as a single-player experience and Rockstar hasn't announced an online mode or a date.

What's confirmed

  • Take-Two's official pre-order announcement (June 24, 2026) says: “Grand Theft Auto VI features a single-player experience.”
  • The PlayStation Store describes GTA 6 as a single-player experience.
  • Rockstar hasn't mentioned any online mode, Push Square noted on November 13, 2026.
  • GTA Online (the GTA V one) carries on: Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said in May 2026 that it will remain on sale.

What's rumor

The most specific claim is unofficial: in September 2026, Twitch CEO Dan Clancy told Bloomberg that multiplayer would launch “next year,” meaning 2027, as reported by VGC. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

Change log

Nov 20, 2026: added Take-Two's official single-player statement. Nov 19, 2026: the game launches without online.

We'll update this page when there's an official announcement.

Sources

  • Take-Two: Rockstar Games announces Grand Theft Auto VI pre-orders (June 24, 2026)
  • VGC: Twitch CEO says GTA 6 multiplayer will launch next year
  • Push Square: GTA 6 doesn't have online multiplayer at launch
  • Wccftech: Take-Two holds the date; Zelnick on PC
  • PlayStation Store listing for GTA VI (Spain)

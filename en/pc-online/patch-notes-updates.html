Are there GTA 6 patches? What we know about update 1.002

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

Yes. Update 1.002.000 arrived before launch, marked as mandatory on PS5 (about 1.07 GB) and about 2.18 GB on Xbox. Rockstar hasn't published patch notes, so what it changes is unknown.

What we know

  • Version: 1.002.000, cited for PS5.
  • Size: about 1.07 GB on PS5 and about 2.18 GB on Xbox Series X|S.
  • Mandatory: KeenGamer calls it a mandatory download, and it's flagged as such on PS5.
  • Patch notes: Rockstar hasn't published any or confirmed it as a “day-one patch.”

Other updates

Gaming press reported a later update, 01.002.001, in mid-November, smaller on PS5. Its changes aren't known either. If you see detailed “fix lists,” be skeptical: they're guesses, not Rockstar's notes.

What to do

Install pending updates before you play. If the game won't start, see GTA 6 won't load, and for space, GTA 6 download size.

Will GTA 6 have mods? What Rockstar's rules say

Reviewed November 20, 2026By Six Stars Editorial TeamPartly confirmed

Short answer

Rockstar has published mod guidelines for all of its games, GTA 6 included: they tolerate single-player, offline, non-commercial mods and restrict expanding the story, porting games or mixing titles. There's no PC version or official mod tools announced.

What's allowed

According to press coverage, the guidelines accept single-player, offline, non-commercial projects that respect third-party intellectual property. Take-Two has said that, in general, it doesn't take legal action against that kind of project in its PC games.

What's restricted

  • Expanding or replacing official missions and story.
  • Unofficial ports and mash-ups between games in the series.
  • Paid and multiplayer mods, which are steered toward the official creator platform built on FiveM.

And GTA 6?

The guidelines are company-wide and aren't an announcement of mod tools for GTA 6. The game is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; for PC, see when GTA 6 is coming to PC. We haven't found a Rockstar text specific to GTA 6 mods.
